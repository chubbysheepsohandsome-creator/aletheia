# ALETHEIA

ALETHEIA analyses English prose for statistical and stylistic patterns associated with AI-generated text.
Everything runs in your browser: documents are never uploaded.

**Free beta. A score is never proof of authorship.** Formal, technical and non-native English writing can show
AI-like patterns, and edited or paraphrased AI text can look human. Do not use a result as the sole basis for any
academic or employment decision.

Open the site: https://chubbysheepsohandsome-creator.github.io/aletheia/

Third-party libraries: jsPDF (MIT), Mammoth (BSD-2-Clause), PDF.js (Apache-2.0); licences are included.
