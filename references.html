Check that the references are real.核查参考文献是否真实存在。

Paste a reference list or a whole document. Each entry is looked up in Crossref and OpenAlex, two open scholarly databases, and marked as found, close, or not found. “Not found” only means a person should check it: books, reports and new papers are often missing from these databases.粘贴参考文献列表或整篇文档。每一条都会到 Crossref 和 OpenAlex 这两个公开学术数据库里查找，并标为找到、接近或未找到。“未找到”只表示需要人工核查：书籍、报告和新论文常常不在这些数据库里。

2. Review Split Entries (1 per blank-line block or line)

Tip: Separate entries with a blank line so multi-line citations stay together.
To check references, only the reference entries (not your document) are sent to Crossref (api.crossref.org) and OpenAlex (api.openalex.org), plus the optional email if you enter one (sent to Crossref only). Like any website, these services also see your IP address. Nothing else leaves your browser.
Note on "Not found": Books, book chapters, reports, grey literature, preprints, very recent publications, and non-English works are frequently absent or incomplete in Crossref and OpenAlex. A "Not found" result is a prompt to verify manually, not proof of fabrication.
