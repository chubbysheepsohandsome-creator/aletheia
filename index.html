01 — Our Philosophy

Language contains more information than the words themselves.

Patterns, structure, consistency and linguistic characteristics can offer valuable insights into how a text may have been produced. ALETHEIA explores these signals through computational analysis.

The name comes from the ancient Greek ἀλήθεια, meaning truth, disclosure, the revealing of what is hidden. Truth is not always visible on the surface; it has to be discovered. That is also why we are careful: a signal is not a verdict, and a score is not proof.

  • Every text has a story.II
  • Beyond detection. Towards understanding.III
  • Discover the patterns behind the words.IV

02 — Technology

Several layers of evidence on a single text.

Trained models make the decision; the interpretable signals below show why. No single hand-picked signal decides the result on its own.

01

Linguistic analysis

Segmentation into prose, quotations, references, headings and lists, so only the author's own running prose is assessed.

02

Statistical text analysis

Sentence-length variation, paragraph uniformity, function-word ratios, punctuation habits and lexical diversity.

03

Machine-learning classification

A gradient-boosted model trained on licensed, labelled data and evaluated on held-out writers and prompts.

04

Multi-model evaluation

Benchmarks across generator families, with whole models held out to test generalisation to systems never seen in training.

05

Writing-style analysis

Template phrases, inflated significance and chatbot artefacts, weighed against signals of genuine human writing.

06

Hybrid authorship analysis

Sentence- and paragraph-level evidence that separates sections, so mixed or edited documents are shown as mixed.

07

Confidence calibration

Thresholds set on calibration data and checked on held-out human writing, with the measured false-positive rates published per group (some groups, such as human answers to instructions, are flagged more often); low evidence is stated plainly.

—

A note on certainty

No single method, and no score, can conclusively establish who wrote a document. ALETHEIA reports patterns and their strength, never proof.

语言包含的信息，比文字本身更多。

模式、结构、一致性和语言特征，可以为一段文本可能是如何写成的提供有价值的线索。ALETHEIA 通过计算分析来探索这些信号。

01

语言分析

把文本分成正文、引文、参考文献、标题和列表，只评估作者自己写的连续正文。

02

统计文本分析

句长变化、段落均匀度、功能词比例、标点习惯和词汇多样性。

03

机器学习分类

用有许可、带标签的数据训练的梯度提升模型，并在预留的写作者和题目上评估。

04

多模型评估

覆盖多个生成器家族的基准测试，整个模型都可以预留出来，测试对训练中从未见过的系统的泛化能力。

05

写作风格分析

套话、夸大的重要性和聊天机器人残留，与真实人写的信号相互权衡。

06

混合作者分析

句子和段落级别的证据能把不同部分分开，所以混合或编辑过的文档会显示为混合。

07

置信度校准

阈值在校准数据上设定，并在留出的人写文本上检验，各群体实测的误判率都会公布（有些群体被标出得更多，例如人写的指令回答）；证据不足时会直接说明。

—

关于确定性

没有任何单一方法或分数能确定一篇文档是谁写的。ALETHEIA 报告的是模式及其强度，但不能证明是谁写的。

Preprocessing separates qualifying prose from headings, lists, tables, mathematical lines, block quotes and reference sections. It removes quotations of six or more words, splits sentences with care for abbreviations and decimals, and checks the language.

Scoring. The engine measures 23 document features. A gradient-boosted tree model trained on licensed, labelled data turns them into a model score. Two thresholds are set on a validation split: the flagging threshold keeps false positives at or below 1% in every human writer group, and the review threshold marks the inconclusive band. Paragraph comparisons use local evidence only, so mixed documents appear mixed.

Deep and Supreme. These scans also run a character- and word-level n-gram model and a segment model that scores windows of about 250 and 100 words, which give the AI percentage and the highlighted passages. Methods and measurements are on the Research page.

What we do not claim

ALETHEIA does not and cannot prove who wrote a document. Its scores are signal strengths on a calibrated scale, not the probability that a particular person used AI. They should never be the only basis for an academic or employment decision.

预处理把合格的正文与标题、列表、表格、数学行、引用块和参考文献分开。它去掉六个词以上的引文，在拆句时注意缩写和小数，并检查语言。

打分。引擎测量 23 个文档特征。一个用有许可、带标签的数据训练的梯度提升树模型把它们转成模型分数。两个阈值在验证集上设定：标出阈值保证每个人写群体的误判不超过 1%，复核阈值划出无法判断的区间。段落之间的比较只用局部证据，所以混合文档会显示为混合。

Deep 和 Supreme。这两种扫描还会运行字符级和词级的 n-gram 模型，以及按约 250 词和 100 词窗口打分的片段模型，用来计算 AI 百分比和高亮片段。方法和测量结果见研究页。

信号，以及我们有多信任它们

文体信号参考了编辑经验总结，例如维基百科的 Signs of AI writing（AI 写作的迹象）。我们把它们当作需要测量的假设，而不是规则。

信号层评级说明
聊天机器人残留（"I hope this helps"、oaicite 标记）文体强除非是引用，人很少会这样写。
套路化模板（"plays a crucial role"、"not just X, it's Y"）文体中等密度比任何单个短语都重要。
与 AI 相关的词汇（"delve"、"tapestry"、"underscores"）文体中等会随着模型变化而漂移，所以词表需要定期更新。
句长过于均匀（突发性低）统计中等认真的正式写作者也可能很均匀。
人写信号（笔误、口语词、小写的"i"）文体中等只会把结果推向"人写"；没有这些信号永远不算 AI 的证据。
词长、功能词、标点、词汇多样性统计单独很弱只组合使用，并按群体检查误判。
语法完美、弯引号、"听起来很正式"—不使用对写作熟练者和非母语写作者不公平。

我们不声称的事

ALETHEIA 不能也无法证明是谁写的。它的分数是校准后刻度上的信号强度，不是某个人使用了 AI 的概率。它们永远不应该成为学术或雇佣决定的唯一依据。

V1.8.4 (test): showing the wording behind a highlight

The AI-percentage model is a linear classifier over word and character n-grams, so a segment’s score splits exactly into one share per n-gram occurrence. We spread each share over the characters it covers and add them up per word; the shares of a single 250-word window plus the intercept reproduce its score to four decimals. Showing raw shares would mostly show topic words, because some of the training text shares topics (in a 6,000-text sample, treaty, keeper’s and ledger carried large shares). So the page only marks words from a fixed list of 283, chosen on a 6,000-text sample (3,000 AI, 3,000 human) of held-out texts: the word must raise segment scores in flagged text from at least six datasets, and appear per document at least twice as often in AI samples as in human samples in most of the six datasets that hold both. Examples: understanding, crucial, ultimately, navigate, insights, comprehensive.

Limits. The list was chosen on the same kind of text it is checked on, so the check below is optimistic. Some listed words are also this year’s task words (cities, reduce in the Perplexity essays), and the list says nothing about Chinese-language text. Human texts that are flagged get marked words too, so the marking shows where the model looks, not who wrote the text. Deleting the marked words lowered the segment score by 1.14 (logit units) on average over 199 AI-written texts, against 0.33 for the same number of random words; on the Perplexity essays the figures were 1.07 and 0.20.

Deep Scan (deep-0.7.0, ALETHEIA V1.0)

Deep and Supreme scans combine a character- and word-level n-gram model with the standard document model. Training text comes from 120+ language models released between 2023 and 2026 (short fiction from public creative-writing benchmarks, including GPT-5.x, Claude 4–5, Gemini 2.5–4, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, Grok, Llama and Mistral), real ChatGPT answers (WildChat), current-model essays, news and abstracts (DACTYL), Q&A (HC3), synthetic textbooks and guides (Cosmopedia), adversarial sets (DetectRL, RAID: paraphrasing, back-translation, synonym swaps, misspellings) and human writing from public-domain fiction, Wikipedia, learner essays and news. Weak spot: older and open models in non-fiction. Wikipedia-style articles, how-to guides and forum answers written by older or small open models (BLOOMz, Dolly, Flan-T5) are rarely detected (0–5%); ChatGPT-written ones are found 12–39% of the time. Human writing in these styles is almost never flagged (0–0.3%).

Open risk: prompt-following human writing. On Beemo, a set of answers written in 2023 by paid annotators to chatbot-style prompts, ALETHEIA V1.0 gives an AI verdict on 12% of the human stories and poems and 16% of the human rewrites (deep-0.4.0: 9% and 16%). These texts score far higher than any other human writing we have tested, so either the detector over-flags this style or some of the "human" answers were written with AI help, a known problem with crowdsourced data. Until we can tell which, treat a positive result on short writing produced to a prompt with extra caution.

What still gets through. Text run through AI "humanizers" and back-translation is detected far less often (back-translation about 30%, DIPPER paraphrasing about 50%, humanized o1-pro news articles 57%), and some highly literary single samples score in the review range rather than above the threshold. We report those as Inconclusive instead of lowering thresholds, because lowering them would flag more human writers.

Where we stand. On short texts from the newest frontier models, independent 2026 benchmarks report Pangram and GPTZero catching about 80% at near-zero false positives, while ALETHEIA's whole-document verdict catches about 21% and its AI percentage (V1.3) flags about a third, more on longer texts. Against humanizers no detector is reliable: on the same outputs GPTZero, Originality and Copyleaks range from 0% to 88% depending on the tool. ALETHEIA runs small statistical models in your browser and never sees your text on a server; closing the gap on frontier models needs a larger server-side model, which is planned. Until then, a low score on a short text is weak evidence of human authorship.

Earlier finding: academic abstracts

deep-0.2.0 flagged 25.7% of 1,834 human academic abstracts in an independent corpus (Mendeley Data, 2025), and the 2023 standard model flagged 11.1%. Both were withdrawn for that register; it now has its own, much stricter threshold.

How other detectors and "humanizers" work

Turnitin splits a submission into overlapping windows of a few hundred words, scores every sentence 0–1 with a fine-tuned transformer classifier built on an open-source foundation model, and averages the scores. It requires at least 300 words, shows scores of 1–19% only as an asterisk, and accepts missing up to about 15% of AI text to keep false positives below 1% on documents with more than 20% AI writing. A second model runs only on segments already flagged as AI and labels those that look AI-paraphrased (QuillBot and similar). Turnitin added "AI bypasser" detection for humanized text in 2025.

Humanizers (HumanizeAI.pro, Undetectable AI, StealthGPT and many more) rewrite AI output to change its statistics. An audit of 19 such tools found synonym swaps, sentence splitting, shifts in reading level, deliberate grammar and punctuation errors, typos, inserted personal pronouns and nonsense insertions; HumanizeAI.pro was among the most fluent. Detectors that rely on perplexity alone lose most of their accuracy on such text, while detectors trained on humanized examples recover most of it, provided human text run through the same tools is also labelled human so the detector learns the authorship signal rather than the rewriting.

Our gap. ALETHEIA runs entirely in your browser, with no transformer language model and no humanized training data from commercial tools. Closing that gap needs a server-side model and licensed humanizer data, which is on the roadmap below.

An honest note on time. A longer scan is more accurate only because it runs more analysis, not because it waits. Deep and Supreme use the same stronger model. Supreme adds 2,000 bootstrap re-analyses, three window sizes and paragraph influence analysis, which make the uncertainty estimate and the section map more reliable, then writes a PDF. On a laptop this takes seconds to about a minute; on a phone or with very long documents, longer.

Caveats. Human samples per group are small, so intervals are wide. Genres we have little human data for (personal essays, blogs, modern published fiction, student creative writing) use the conservative threshold, and their true false-positive rate is unmeasured. The Deep model was trained on research data whose upstream rights are still under review, so it must be retrained on commercially cleared data before a paid launch.

V1.8.4（测试）：显示高亮背后的用词

AI 百分比模型是建立在词和字符 n-gram 上的线性分类器，所以一个片段的分数可以精确拆成每个 n-gram 出现位置的一份贡献。我们把每份贡献摊到它覆盖的字符上，再按词加总；单个 250 词窗口的各词贡献加上截距，和该窗口的分数一致到小数点后四位。直接显示这些贡献主要会显示题目词，因为部分训练文本的题目相同（在 6,000 篇样本里，treaty、keeper’s、ledger 的贡献很大）。所以页面只标出固定名单里的 283 个词。这个名单是在 6,000 篇预留文本样本（3,000 篇 AI、3,000 篇人写）上挑出的：词必须在至少六个数据集的被标文本里拉高片段分数，并且在同时含有两类文本的六个数据集中的大多数里，每篇出现的频率在 AI 样本中至少是人写样本的两倍。例子：understanding、crucial、ultimately、navigate、insights、comprehensive。

局限。名单是在和检验同类的文本上挑的，所以下面的检验结果偏乐观。名单里有些词也是今年的任务词（Perplexity 作文里的 cities、reduce），名单也不涉及中文文本。被判为 AI 的人写文本同样会有被标记的词，所以标记只表示模型在看哪里，不表示是谁写的。删掉标出的词，199 篇 AI 文本的片段分数平均下降 1.14（对数几率单位），随机删掉同样数量的词只下降 0.33；Perplexity 作文分别是 1.07 和 0.20。

未解决的风险：按提示写作的人写文本。Beemo 是 2023 年由付费标注员按聊天机器人式提示写的回答集。ALETHEIA V1.0 把其中 12% 的人写故事和诗歌、16% 的人工改写判为 AI（deep-0.4.0：9% 和 16%）。这些文本的分数远高于我们测过的任何其他人写文本，所以要么检测器对这种风格过度敏感，要么部分"人写"回答其实用了 AI，这是众包数据的已知问题。在我们弄清楚之前，对按提示写成的短文给出的阳性结果要格外谨慎。

仍然漏掉的。经过 AI "人性化"工具和回译处理的文本检出率低得多（回译约 30%，DIPPER 改写约 50%，人性化处理过的 o1-pro 新闻 57%），一些文学性很强的单篇样本落在复核区间而不是超过阈值。我们把这些报告为无法判断，而不是降低阈值，因为降低阈值会误判更多人写作者。

AI 百分比与高亮片段（seg-0.2，ALETHEIA V1.2，已被取代）

Deep 和 Supreme 扫描会报告一个 AI 百分比：看起来像 AI 生成的部分中合格词所占的比例，这是 Turnitin 推广的衡量方式。文档被切成约 250 词、相互重叠的片段；片段模型给每个片段打分，每个句子取它所属片段的平均分。1% 到 19% 的值像 Turnitin 一样显示为 *%，因为这个区间更容易误判。全文的 Deep 分数仍会显示；两者回答的是不同的问题。

我们的现状。在最新前沿模型写的短文上，独立的 2026 年基准报告 Pangram 和 GPTZero 能在误判接近零的情况下抓到约 80%，而 ALETHEIA 的全文判断约为 21%，AI 百分比（V1.3）能标出约三分之一，长文会更多。面对人性化工具，没有哪个检测器可靠：在同一批输出上，GPTZero、Originality 和 Copyleaks 根据工具不同在 0% 到 88% 之间。ALETHEIA 在你的浏览器里运行小型统计模型，服务器从不接触你的文本；要缩小与前沿模型的差距，需要更大的服务器端模型，这在计划中。在那之前，短文得分低只是"人写"的弱证据。

早期发现：学术摘要

deep-0.2.0 在一个独立语料库（Mendeley Data，2025）中把 1,834 篇人写学术摘要的 25.7% 判为 AI，2023 年的标准模型判了 11.1%。两者都在这个文体上撤回；现在它有自己的、严格得多的阈值。

其他检测器和"人性化"工具如何工作

Turnitin 把提交的文本切成几百词、相互重叠的窗口，用一个基于开源基础模型微调的 transformer 分类器给每个句子打 0–1 分，然后取平均。它要求至少 300 词，1–19% 的分数只显示为星号，并接受最多漏掉约 15% 的 AI 文本，以保证在 AI 写作超过 20% 的文档上误判率低于 1%。第二个模型只在已被标为 AI 的片段上运行，标出看起来经过 AI 改写的部分（QuillBot 之类）。Turnitin 在 2025 年增加了针对人性化文本的"AI 绕过工具"检测。

人性化工具（HumanizeAI.pro、Undetectable AI、StealthGPT 等很多）会改写 AI 输出以改变其统计特征。一项对 19 款此类工具的审计发现：同义词替换、拆句、改变阅读难度、故意制造语法和标点错误、拼写错误、插入人称代词和无意义内容；HumanizeAI.pro 是最流畅的之一。只靠困惑度的检测器在这类文本上大部分准确度都会丧失，而用人性化样本训练过的检测器能恢复大部分，前提是经过同样工具处理的人写文本也标为人写，这样检测器学到的是作者信号，而不是改写痕迹。

我们的差距。ALETHEIA 完全在你的浏览器里运行，没有 transformer 语言模型，也没有来自商业工具的人性化训练数据。缩小这个差距需要服务器端模型和有许可的人性化数据，已列入下面的路线图。

关于时间的说明。扫描更久更准确，只是因为它做了更多分析，不是因为它在等待。Deep 和 Supreme 用同一个更强的模型。Supreme 额外做 2,000 次自助法（bootstrap）重分析、三种窗口尺寸和段落影响分析，使不确定度估计和段落分布图更可靠，然后生成 PDF。在笔记本电脑上这需要几秒到约一分钟；在手机上或文档很长时会更久。

注意事项。每个群体的人写样本较少，所以区间较宽。我们人写数据很少的文体（个人随笔、博客、现代出版小说、学生创意写作）使用保守阈值，真实误判率尚未测量。Deep 模型用的研究数据，其上游版权仍在审查中，所以在付费上线前必须用已获商业许可的数据重新训练。

上线门槛

一个模型只有在以下条件都满足时才会上线：它的训练文本没有出现在评估数据中，整体误判的人写文本不超过 1%。任何变差的写作群体，或误判超过 2% 的群体，都会随版本公布（V1.9.5：人写的指令回答，65 篇中 11 篇，17%）。

V1.9.8 26 Nov 2026 · similarity check against web sources with a PDF report, much more detailed Writing Process

  • Similarity check on the Detect page. Choose “Similarity” next to Deep Scan and Supreme. ALETHEIA picks up to 40 phrases of 8 words from the text and searches Wikipedia for each (and OpenAlex's full-text index of scholarly works for up to 15 of them, to stay within its small free daily limit). It then compares the text word for word with the Wikipedia pages it finds (up to 12) and with the abstracts of the papers; a phrase found in a paper's full text counts as an 8-word match. Passages of 8 or more words that match are highlighted and numbered by source, and each word is credited to one source only, so the overall percentage never counts a word twice.
  • A full similarity report. Overall similarity, match groups (not cited or quoted, cited but not quoted, quoted but not cited, cited and quoted), top sources by type, a numbered source list with links, the whole text with coloured matches, and a downloadable PDF in the same layout. Filters recompute the result without searching again: exclude the bibliography, exclude quoted text, and the smallest match (8, 12 or 20 words).
  • Failures are shown, not hidden. If more than half of the online searches fail (network problems or a service's daily limit), the result and the PDF are marked incomplete instead of showing a clean score.
  • Integrity flags. The report also counts zero-width characters, unusual spaces and words that mix Latin with look-alike Greek or Cyrillic letters. For Word files it also counts hidden, white and tiny text. A flag points to something worth a look; it is not proof of anything.
  • Documents saved on this device. You can save a checked document in this browser. Later checks also compare against saved documents, for example a class's earlier submissions. Saved documents never leave the browser.
  • Privacy. Only the short phrases are sent, to wikipedia.org and api.openalex.org, and only when “Search Wikipedia and OpenAlex” is ticked. The whole document never leaves your browser.
  • Limits. This is not a Turnitin replacement. It does not search the whole web, paywalled articles or other students' work in private databases, and only a limited number of phrases are searched online, so a low score does not mean the text is original and the percentage is not comparable with Turnitin's.
  • Writing Process, much more detail. New sections: session by session (words each editing session left in the file), formatting and source clues (main font and size, paragraphs in a different font or size, spelling-language tags such as English (Australia) and English (US), hidden, white or tiny text, highlighting), revisions and comments by day, and structure and characters (headings, tables, images, links, footnotes, equations, paragraph and sentence length, curly and straight quotes, dashes, unusual spaces, Markdown marks, linked websites). New observations explain each clue with ordinary reasons. Clicking a differently formatted paragraph shows it in the paragraph map. The exported summary includes the new details.
  • Detector unchanged. The AI detector models and thresholds are the same as V1.9.7, so AI percentages do not change.

V1.9.8 2026 年 11 月 26 日 · 和网络来源比对的相似度检查（带 PDF 报告），写作过程显示更详细

  • 检测页上的相似度检查。在深度扫描和至尊旁边选择“相似度”。ALETHEIA 从文本里挑出最多 40 个短语（每个 8 个词），逐个在 Wikipedia 里搜索（其中最多 15 个也在 OpenAlex 的学术全文索引里搜索，以免超出它很小的每日免费额度）。然后把文本和找到的 Wikipedia 页面（最多 12 个）以及论文摘要逐词比对；在论文全文里找到的短语算作 8 个词的匹配。至少 8 个词相同的片段会被标出颜色、按来源编号；每个词只算给一个来源，所以总百分比不会重复计算同一个词。
  • 完整的相似度报告。包括总相似度、匹配分组（没有引用也没加引号、有引用但没加引号、加了引号但没引用、有引用也加了引号）、按类型的主要来源、带链接的编号来源列表、标出匹配的全文，以及同样排版、可下载的 PDF。筛选项不用重新搜索就能重新计算：排除参考文献、排除引号内文字、最短匹配（8、12 或 20 词）。
  • 失败会明确显示。如果一半以上的在线搜索失败（网络问题或服务的每日限额），结果和 PDF 都会标为“不完整”，而不是显示一个看似干净的分数。
  • 完整性标记。报告还会统计零宽字符、特殊空格，以及把拉丁字母和外形相似的希腊或西里尔字母混在一起的单词。对 Word 文件，还会统计隐藏、白色和极小字号的文字。标记只是提示值得看一看，不证明任何事。

Questions

Is 100/100 proof that AI wrote it? No. Scores describe how closely the writing resembles AI-generated text the models have seen. Human writing in some styles (formal, formulaic, prompt-following) can score high.

Why does it say "Inconclusive"? Because the evidence is not strong enough either way, or because the writing style (for example academic abstracts) is one where AI and human text are hard to tell apart. We prefer saying so to guessing.

Can AI "humanizers" fool it? Often, yes. Paraphrased and humanized AI text is detected far less reliably (see Research). ALETHEIA does undo invisible characters and look-alike letters that some tools insert.

Which languages work? English prose only, at least 80 words; 300+ words gives more reliable results.

Why do Deep Scan and Supreme results differ slightly? Supreme runs more bootstrap re-analyses and several window sizes, so its interval is estimated more precisely and can differ slightly; the AI percentage uses the same thresholds in both.

常见问题

100/100 能证明是 AI 写的吗？不能。分数描述的是这段文字与模型见过的 AI 生成文本有多相似。某些风格的人写文本（正式、套路化、按提示写作）也可能得分很高。

为什么显示"无法判断"？因为证据在两个方向上都不够强，或者这种写作风格（例如学术摘要）中 AI 和人写文本很难区分。我们宁愿直说，也不去猜。

AI "人性化"工具能骗过它吗？经常可以。改写过和人性化处理过的 AI 文本检出的可靠性低得多（见研究页）。ALETHEIA 会还原某些工具插入的不可见字符和形近字母。

支持哪些语言？只支持英文正文，至少 80 词；300 词以上结果更可靠。

为什么 Deep Scan 和 Supreme 的结果略有不同？Supreme 做更多次自助法重分析，并使用几种窗口尺寸，所以它的区间估计更精确，可能略有不同；两者的 AI 百分比使用相同的阈值。
